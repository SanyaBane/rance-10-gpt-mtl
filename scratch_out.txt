Demon Activation 　Although gods no longer appear due to the God 　Anomaly, demons continue to appear as before, and have become more noticeable with the absence of gods.  　Demon Neplacus begins scheming to cause even more 　chaos in the world. Hero Geimark Returns 　Hero Geimark, thought to have died in the Hero Disaster 　3 years ago, appears. Due to the God Anomaly, no 　hero succession occurred, turning him into an unkillable monster. New Year's Gathering of Rance's Children 　Reset leads the planning of a New Year's gathering for 　Rance's children and their parents. After this, it becomes an 　annual event, though some years it cannot be held. 　Since most parents are national leaders, the gathering also carries great political significance, but separately, everyone enjoyed a brief moment of peace together.  　Demon King Rance was also invited, but did not appear.

Demon King Rance's New Fiend 　On a whim, Demon King Rance gives his blood and 　creates 4 new Fiends. Fiend Neru Apollo, Fiend 　Heavy Calorie, Fiend Walport, Fiend DD. Though their 　personalities differ completely, these dangerous Fiends who all enjoy combat and massacre fight the New Twelve Knights and greatly torment them.

Using the most vicious 　creature SBR to stop Demon King Rance's movements, they succeed in Reset's slap. Thus, they finally succeed in restoring Rance to sanity once again.  　During this battle, the demon sword Chaos is blown away and goes missing.

Demon's Battle 　Demon Neplacus continues to target Dark Rance 　whenever there is an opportunity.
